# libSceAgcDriver

## add Functional

- [Waits ordered by the epoch's start](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Synchronization/WaitStatistics.cpp) (libSceAgcDriver) - a `WAIT_REG_MEM` that guest memory satisfied starts no collect epoch when nothing was stored over the awaited flag since the worker's epoch began: no CPU store to its 4 KiB page, no driver store over its bytes (the whole 64 KiB block once more than four ranges of it were stored since) and no recorded GPU write pending over it. A stamp is the generation of the walk that collected a store, not of the store, so a flag stored before the epoch and first collected after it still starts one; the worker therefore collects the pages of the flags it awaited (four pages; at the next three bumps after a wait that started no epoch, at the next one after a wait that did) before it bumps, one write-watch call each, whether or not a wait on them follows. A GPU store stamped only when its batch is reaped (a shader's output, not a label) is covered by its pending-write note alone. Written without a build: not compiled, `agc_collect_memo_generation` and `agc_driver_same_queue_label_wait` not run, not measured. `APS5_NO_EPOCH_ORDERED_WAITS=1` restores the bump and stops the collects. Closes when: built, tested and measured in a title.
- [Ordered-wait memo on waits memory did not satisfy](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Synchronization/WaitStatistics.cpp) (libSceAgcDriver) - the memo of `APS5_NO_ORDERED_WAITS` compares guest memory at the awaited address for every wait that reaches it, including one that passed on the value it held at submit or on another queue's label not yet stored in memory, where memory says nothing about the store that satisfied the wait: such a wait can skip the epoch that the other queue's submission needs. Queue 0 does not land those waits before its next draw. Labels: code, no-test.
- [Epochs at interrupts, drains and reaps](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Packets/PacketMemory.cpp) (libSceAgcDriver) - the collect epoch advances at every interrupting end of pipe, device drain and reap outside a submission. None of them orders a CPU write before a later command (a submission or a wait the CPU satisfied does), but a memoized collect made while completions are reaped would otherwise be answered by a walk older than the reap, and which of those collects need a fresh walk is not established. `[collects]` counts these bumps by reason. Closes when: the collects made under a reap are listed.
