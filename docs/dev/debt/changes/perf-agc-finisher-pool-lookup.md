# libSceAgcDriver

## add Opt-in draw paths

- [Draw lookup on the finisher pool](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Draw/Draw.cpp) (`APS5_FINISHER_LOOKUP` with `APS5_FINISHER_THREADS` above 1) - opt-in, written without a build: not compiled, and not run in its tests, on a replay of captured frames or in a title. The lookup of a draw runs beside the jobs queued before it, so a lookup that reads a range one of them writes waits for its turn and for the committer, and which variant of an entry a hit promotes, and which hit moves an entry in the eviction order, depend on the order the lookups finish in. `APS5_VERIFY_FINISHER_LOOKUP` compares and counts and does not fail the driver. Closes when: measured in a title.
- [Draw-cache lookups counted without the lock](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Draw/DrawLookup.cpp) - on by default, written without a build: not compiled, and not run in its tests, on a replay of captured frames or in a title. A lookup that finds the draw cache lock held does not promote the variant it matched nor refresh its entry's place in the eviction order; a later hit of the entry does, so an entry hit only while the lock is held can be evicted earlier than with `APS5_NO_UNBLOCKED_DRAW_HITS`. How often a lookup finds the lock held is counted on the `[finisher-lookup]` line and not measured. Closes when: measured in a title.
- [Locks the finisher pool's threads share](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Draw/DrawPipeline.cpp) (`APS5_FINISHER_THREADS`) - each validation of a variant on a pool thread takes the finisher's mutex and the committer's to look for writes queued ahead, and each range handed to the collect walker takes the walker's producer mutex; the waits for the first two are not counted. The draw cache lock is still taken once for a draw served by its shape and once to find the recipe of an exact hit. Not measured.
