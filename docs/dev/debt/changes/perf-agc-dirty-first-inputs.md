# libSceAgcDriver

## add Opt-in draw paths

- [Dirty-first draw inputs](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Graphics/src/BufferCache.cpp) (`APS5_DIRTY_FIRST_INPUTS`) - opt-in and written without a build: it has not been compiled, run in its test or measured in a title. Closes when: built, its test passes and a title is measured with it and with `APS5_VERIFY_DIRTY_FIRST_INPUTS`.
- [Dirty-first runs of draws](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Graphics/src/BufferCache.cpp) (`APS5_DIRTY_FIRST_INPUTS`) - a range copied or uploaded for one draw is served again to the later draws of the same run without looking at guest memory, on the assumption that a title rewrites what submitted draws read only after it saw an effect of work ordered behind them; the verification mode only counts the ranges that break it. A label the GPU stores is taken to be invisible before its batch is submitted. A dispatch, a submission end and a buffer fill still end the run at their commit: the same argument covers them, but their bodies were not read for it. Draws committed outside the pipeline end the run at every draw. Not compiled, not measured: the count of draws per run is unknown.
- [Dirty-first remaining collects](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Graphics/src/Draw.cpp) (`APS5_DIRTY_FIRST_INPUTS`) - a vertex range under a pending GPU write still takes the GPU copy path, whose orders collect the write watch of their range once. Arming a block resets its write watch with one system call besides the protection change. On the path of a draw from a recipe the collects left are those of a surface whose blocks cannot be armed or were written, the lookups of a full walk of the template, the refresh of a render target that changed, the store-back checks of a cleared depth surface and the layers a draw marks pending; the legacy proof path (`APS5_NO_EPOCH_REVALIDATE`) and the write index (`APS5_TABLE_INDEX`) collect as before.
- [Dirty-first armed proofs](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/GuestMemory.cpp) (`APS5_DIRTY_FIRST_INPUTS`) - the sweep of a resource version and the collect of a proved surface are answered from the words of armed blocks, after one uncached collect at the arming; written without a build and not measured. A range of more than 4096 blocks is never armed for a proof, a refused range is asked again only 256 collect epochs later, and neither bound is configurable or measured. Every template keeps its own tokens for a surface, so a surface shared by many templates is collected once per template at the arming. A write of the GPU into an armed imported block that is not announced leaves the proof standing, as it leaves the block armed (`APS5_VERIFY_PAGE_TRACK` reports it). Closes when: built, its test passes and a title is measured with it and with `APS5_VERIFY_DIRTY_FIRST_INPUTS`.
- [Dirty-first ring](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Graphics/src/BufferCache.cpp) (`APS5_DIRTY_FIRST_INPUTS`) - the ring grows to 64 chunks of 4 MiB outside the cache's 1 GiB budget and never shrinks; neither bound is configurable or measured. A range that reads at most the ring size but starts more than 1 MiB past its address is protected and mirrored instead. When every chunk is still read by unfinished batches, and when the mirror refuses a range (pages an unfinished batch reads, a pending GPU write, a label, an offset alignment the zone does not keep), the range is copied to a device buffer of its own, allocated for that draw. The copies are made by the committer under the GPU lock.
