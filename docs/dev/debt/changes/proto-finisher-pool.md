# libSceAgcDriver

## add Opt-in draw paths

- [Draw finisher pool](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Draw/DrawPipeline.cpp) (`APS5_FINISHER_THREADS`) - opt-in, written without a build: not compiled, and not run in its tests, on a replay of captured frames or in a title. The jobs of several finisher threads run side by side and a reorder buffer hands their items to the committer in the order the queue worker queued the jobs; a job queued without the ranges it writes (a draw whose stages may store to guest memory beyond its targets) runs alone, after every job queued before it. A job no longer finds the items of the jobs queued before it in the committer's queue: its captures never read that queue, and the read ranges of an item a finished job still keeps hold write-backs back from the finisher's list (`DrawFinisher::DeferredReads`) as they do from the queue. Draw cache entries, shape keys, stage sightings and recipes are inserted in the order the jobs finish, so which entry an eviction or an admission keeps can differ from a run with one finisher. The pool's threads share one trace name, and only the first reserves a core and is sampled by `APS5_SAMPLE_FINISHER`. Closes when: measured in a title.
- [Collect walker producers](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Execution/src/Driver/Draw/DrawPipeline.cpp) (`APS5_FINISHER_THREADS`) - the pool's threads hand their ranges to the collect walker under one mutex, in the order they reach it: the ranges of a later draw can precede those of an earlier one, and the walker then collects the earlier draw's ranges in the later draw's collect epoch, which walks at least as often. Not measured.
